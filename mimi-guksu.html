Our Beginning

Warmth in every bowl

Mimi Guksu was born from a love of Korean noodle culture — the street stalls, the family kitchens, the steaming bowls that bring comfort on any day.

Our Philosophy

A bowl of noodles is more than a meal. It is warmth, comfort, and home.

Our broths simmer for over 12 hours. Our noodles are made fresh every morning. Every ingredient is chosen with intention — from the anchovy and kelp in our stock to the seasonal vegetables that top each bowl.

Our Experience

A place to slow down.

In a world that moves too fast, Mimi Guksu is a place to pause. Sit down, breathe in the steam rising from your bowl, and take your time.

What Sets Us Apart

Built on three commitments

01 — Broth

Slow-Simmered Broth

Slow-Simmered Broth

Our broths are simmered for over 12 hours using beef bones, anchovies, and kelp for deep, layered flavor that forms the soul of every bowl.

02 — Noodles

Fresh Noodles

Fresh Noodles

Our noodles are made in-house daily — hand-pulled and cut for the perfect chewy texture. We never use pre-made noodles because the difference is in every bite.

03 — Space

Comfort Dining

Comfort Dining

A warm, inviting space designed for lingering over a hot bowl. Wooden counters, soft lighting, and the gentle sound of broth bubbling.
