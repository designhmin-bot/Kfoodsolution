Our Beginning

Where fire meets tradition

Oiso was born from a simple belief: that the best meals happen around an open flame, shared with people you care about. We set out to recreate the warmth of Korean BBQ culture for a new generation.

Our Philosophy

Every cut is selected with care. Every flame is watched with patience.

We source premium meats daily from trusted suppliers. Our charcoal is selected for clean, even heat. And our banchan is prepared fresh every morning, following recipes passed down through generations of Korean home cooks.

Our Experience

More than a meal. A gathering.

At Oiso, every table becomes a stage. You grill, you share, you connect. Our staff guides you through the experience — from the first sizzle to the last wrap — so you can focus on the people around you.

What Sets Us Apart

Built on three commitments

01 — Sourcing

Quality Cuts

Quality Cuts

Hand-selected premium meats sourced daily from trusted farms. We inspect every cut to ensure the marbling, freshness, and flavor our guests expect at every visit.

02 — Tradition

Traditional Banchan

Traditional Banchan

Complimentary side dishes prepared in-house every morning. From kimchi aged in-house to hand-made pickled radish, our banchan honors the time-tested recipes of Korean home cooking.

03 — Experience

Live Grill

Live Grill

Tabletop charcoal grilling for the full interactive Korean BBQ experience. Our staff guides you through every step — choosing cuts, managing heat, and building the perfect ssam wrap.
